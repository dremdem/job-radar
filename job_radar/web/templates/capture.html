Send a LinkedIn page to Job Radar

Drag this link to your bookmarks bar: → Job Radar

  1. Open a LinkedIn job search (e.g. an alert's “See all jobs”) and scroll the list to the end of the first page, or open one vacancy.
  2. Click → Job Radar in the bookmarks bar. A tab opens here and shows what was received.

If a page comes back “unreadable”: also drag → Job Radar (debug), open the same LinkedIn page, click it once. It saves the page's HTML locally in data/capture_debug/ so the parser can be fixed; nothing is sent anywhere else.

The bookmark only reads the page you opened; it never clicks, scrolls or sends requests to LinkedIn. Details: docs/linkedin-capture.md.

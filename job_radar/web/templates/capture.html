Capture

Send a LinkedIn or hh.ru page you opened to Job Radar. The bookmark only reads that page; it never clicks, scrolls or sends requests to the site. Details: docs/linkedin-capture.md.

{# the result at a glance: green / yellow / red (#219) #}
Set up once

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

→ Job Radar
Each time
  1. Open a LinkedIn job search (e.g. an alert's “See all jobs”) and scroll the list to the end of the first page, or open one vacancy. On hh.ru, open a vacancy page.
  2. Click → Job Radar in the bookmarks bar. A tab opens here, shows what was received and closes itself.
If a page comes back “unreadable”

Also drag → Job Radar (debug), open the same page, click it once. It saves the page's HTML locally in data/capture_debug/ so the parser can be fixed; nothing is sent anywhere else.
